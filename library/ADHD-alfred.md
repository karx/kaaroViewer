\# ADHD Assistant Bot — Input Processing, Output Nudges, and Demo Scope with Hermes \*\*Date:\*\* Sun, Jul 19, 2026 \#\# The Core Problem: ADHD Executive Dysfunction \- ADHD creates compounding productivity failures: \- Hard to prioritize tasks by urgency, intensity, and time required \- Executive dysfunction: knows what to do, but can't start \- Context switches constantly across 4-5 meetings/day and multiple workstreams \- Tends to drift to low-priority Slack/backlog instead of most important task \- Body doubling works (working sessions with others), but doesn't scale \- Existing tools (Granola, pendants, PKM) capture context but never nudge or push back \- Pendant analogy: "It just takes in, like my Granola. There's no output, no nudge." \#\# Vision: Physical Chief of Staff for ADHD \- A dedicated physical device, not a laptop tab, because: \- A laptop tab becomes another ADHD distraction vector \- Physical device has one job: humanization of a chief of staff \- Chief of staff characteristics to build around: \- Highly organized, highly proactive \- Strong instinct for what you need before you ask \- Surfaces only the 3 things requiring human input; everything else in motion \- Personality as an outcome, not a setting: evolves from your notes and behavior over time \- Starter templates (e.g., Harvey Specter from Suits) that evolve with the user \- Longer-term: digital twin of the user's ideal self, scoring gap between current and ideal state \#\# Input Sources \- Meeting context: Granola (online) \+ physical world audio capture \- Workspace: Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams \- Email: Gmail, Outlook \- Calendar: real-time integration, auto-adds and reschedules meetings \- Knowledge base / PKM: Notion, Obsidian, or manual markdown \- Passive sensors (additional, composable): \- Wearables, pendants for health data (sleep, exercise) \- Sleep quality linked to ADHD trigger prediction \#\# Processing and Output Design \- Processing layers: \- Worldview construction: all inputs pushed into a unified worldview; prioritization anchored to projects you care about \- GTD/GST cycle: daily half-hour session to capture, triage, and delegate tasks vocally \- Delegation engine: what does the agent handle vs. what requires the human \- Calendar reprioritization: when a new meeting lands, auto-reschedules displaced tasks \- ADHD coping skills as composable agent skills (sourced from psychology research) \- Output split into two modes: \- Historical: daily summary, current to-do list, nudges computed from context \- Real-time: active task tracking, what's running in the background right now \- Nudge mechanism must be seamless: \- Voice check-in every 1-2 hours ("bro, where are you?") \- User responds verbally; bot updates plan and reprioritizes \- Hardware actuators for physical feedback (buzzer, LED, movement) \- Gratification loop on task completion (physical signal, or ordering a reward) \- Scoring metric: deadlines pushed per week, not tasks completed \- Every task must have a deadline; a task without one is just a hope \#\# Demo Scope and Build Plan \- Demo wow factor: bot is live on stage, picks up context when presenter walks out, confirms the three items to cover \- Composability as a key message: hardware, software, and inputs all swappable \- Extensibility pitch: not just ADHD, applicable to all neurodivergent profiles (autism, depression, etc.) \- Available hardware: buzzer, hall sensor, LCD 16x2 display, movement sensor \- Architecture: everything runs locally on one machine; Hermes as the agent brain; Cortex as the reasoning layer \- Build approach: start with basics now, reassess in 1-2 hours \#\# Next Steps \- \*\*Scope and divide build tasks\*\* — Split input capture, processing (GTD cycle, calendar, delegation), and output (nudges, daily summary) across the team before starting to build. \- \*\*Compile ADHD coping skills as agent skills\*\* (Akash) — Research established ADHD management techniques from psychology literature and package them as composable bot skills. \- \*\*Define demo flow and wow moment\*\* — Work backward from the on-stage demo: bot live, picks up context on entry, confirms three agenda items in real time.
