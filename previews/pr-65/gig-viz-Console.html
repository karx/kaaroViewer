§08 CONCLUSION — PATH FORWARD

The "10-minute" delivery model is built on a foundation of human and ergonomic exploitation. The delivery partner in India exists in a state of "algorithmic panopticon" — every second tracked, every movement scrutinized, every failure penalized. While 2025 interventions provide a legislative framework, they do not yet address the immediate crisis on the ground.

MANDATE 01
Guarantee minimum wage accounting for the 32% overhead cost of fuel and maintenance
MANDATE 02
Require algorithmic transparency on route assignment logic and pay fluctuation
MANDATE 03
Standardize safety gear provision — vibration dampening and ergonomic tools — as platform obligation, not worker expense
BOTTOM-UP SURVIVAL STRATEGYACTIONABLE NOW

In the absence of corporate shifts, 3D-printed ergonomic tools provide a crucial bottom-up survival mechanism for the 10+ million riders who form the backbone of urban commerce. By using technology to "fit the task to the person" rather than the person to the task, the physical erosion of the workforce can be slowed — even as the larger fight for labor dignity continues in the courts and on the streets.
