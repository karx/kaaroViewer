General Print Guide

These parts will be used outdoors, in rain, in 40°C heat, and under mechanical stress for 12+ hours daily. Standard print-quality shortcuts will cause premature failure. Follow these principles.

🌡️

Dry Your Filament

TPU and Nylon absorb moisture aggressively. Wet filament produces bubbling, weak layer bonds, and stringing. Dry at 60–65°C for 4h before any TPU print.

🐢

Slow Down for TPU

TPU at >35 mm/s creates under-extrusion and inconsistent walls. 20–30 mm/s is not optional — it is the spec. Disable retraction or set it to <1mm.

🧱

Walls Over Infill

For structural parts (LS-001, LS-002), 5 perimeter walls with 40% infill is stronger than 2 walls with 80% infill. Layer bonding on the outer shell is what resists shock loads.

🌀

Gyroid = Best Infill

Gyroid distributes stress isotropically in 3D. For vibration-absorbing parts (grips, throttle assist), it outperforms grid, honeycomb, or cubic at equal density.

☀️

ASA Needs Enclosure

ASA is the only material that won't deform in prolonged 40°C sun (Tg ≈ 95°C vs PETG's 70°C). But it warps severely without an enclosed print chamber and 90–100°C bed.

🔧

Test Fit at 0 Stress

All mounting parts (throttle assist, stabilizers) must be test-fit at standstill first. Mechanical load will not forgive a slightly loose bore or misaligned bracket.
